About

From the flight line to the SOC

After the Marines I worked in logistics and quality control: warehouse team lead at IKEA, ammunition quality control at National Police Ammunition, and inventory at Ashley Furniture, where I kept more than $5M in stock tracked with RF scanning. Each job came down to accurate records, change control, and audit readiness.

I went back to school to make that discipline technical. Across two Full Sail degrees I built networks for a fictional law firm, wrote a full NIST 800-53 security program for a fictional retailer, scanned and patched lab networks with Nessus, and automated deployments with Ansible, Docker and AWS.

Today I tutor Full Sail cybersecurity students through RMF, vulnerability assessment, Windows and Linux hardening, and lab troubleshooting. I'm studying for CompTIA Security+.

Case 01 · Project & Portfolio VIISecurity

From discovery to defense: a full vulnerability-management cycle

I took a lab network for "HammerCorp" from an unknown state to a patched one. I inventoried every VM, ran non-credentialed and credentialed Nessus scans, opened remediation tickets for each finding, patched, and rescanned to confirm the fixes.

Asset discovery

Built an inventory of IP, hostname, OS and version, RAM, disk, services and purpose for each machine: OPNsense firewall, Windows Server 2016 mail server, Rocky Linux Nessus host, Splunk server and Windows clients.

Scanning

Compared unauthenticated and authenticated results to show how much a credentialed scan reveals, and how much it can rule out.

Ticket & verify

Documented each vulnerability, its risk and the fix, tracked start and close times, and confirmed closure on rescan.

SnowBe Online security program

Case 03 · Threat Protection & Testing · Secure Systems and DataSecurity

Attack, then remediate

In isolated labs I exploited known weaknesses from Kali so I could understand them, then closed them and proved it with a scan.

  • Tested DVWA and rated severity by RCE, access needed, authentication and active exploitation

Case 04 · Project & Portfolio I, II, IV (IT)Infrastructure

Marconi Law, LLC: one client across three courses

As the consultant for my fictional company PrimeTech IT, I carried one client through design, build and support. Marconi Law had grown to 10 employees spread across three floors and needed one consolidated, expandable network.

Design · Project & Portfolio I

Scoped the project and planned a 10.10.119.0/24 network with static addressing for 28 nodes: workstations, VoIP phones, server, switch, two WAPs, printer and conference room. Wrote the Windows Server 2019 user and group build procedures. Estimated 5 weeks and $42,854.51.

Build · Project & Portfolio II

Built a 104-page proof of concept for the firm's website. Ran Ghost in Docker behind an Nginx reverse proxy on Rocky 8, set up WordPress on an Ubuntu LAMP stack, then hardened it: file permissions, a locked-down wp-config.php, and documented vulnerability, configuration and validation for each control.

Support · Project & Portfolio IV

Wrote a 12-month service level agreement covering monitoring, patching and response times. Produced an asset inventory and help-desk reports (incidents by date, technician, department and priority) and presented an IT governance briefing.
